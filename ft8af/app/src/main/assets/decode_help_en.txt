Decode mode:
Fast decode: One decoding pass on received audio.
Balanced decode: Adds a deeper decoding pass that tries harder on weak signals, without the extra passes below.
Deep decode: Default mode. Builds on Balanced decode with additional passes that strip out already-decoded signals and re-decode the rest, recovering weaker signals hidden underneath stronger ones.
* Note: Balanced and Deep decoding require longer decoding time and more battery consumption, with Deep the most of the two.
